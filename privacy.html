Privacy Policy

Media Studio · Effective 2026-10-13

Who we are

Media Studio is a private tool that the operator of the Blip & Friends channels runs on their own computer to publish the channels' own videos to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok and X. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

Data controller: the private individual who runs the Blip & Friends channels, the Netherlands. Contact: highvibes101.101@gmail.com.

Data we access

Media Studio only signs in to the operator's own accounts, and only with the permissions below. It never accesses viewers' data, comments, followers, messages, analytics, email or contacts.

YouTube (Google)

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy, "made for kids" and AI-disclosure settings.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to look up the channel's most recent uploads, so that a video is never uploaded twice after an interrupted upload.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta)

  • pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management: to find the Blip & Friends Facebook Page the operator selects, and the Instagram account linked to it.
  • instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish: to read the Instagram account's ID and username, publish its own Reels, and check its recent posts so a Reel is never posted twice.
  • pages_manage_posts: to publish and schedule the Page's own Reels and check its recent Reels for the same reason.

Threads (Meta)

  • threads_basic and threads_content_publish: to read the Threads account's ID and username, publish its own posts, and check its recent posts so a post is never published twice.

TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, and through the posting API its username, the visibility options it allows and its comment, duet and stitch settings.
  • video.publish: to post the account's own videos and check whether a post finished processing.

X

  • users.read: the account's ID and username.
  • tweet.write and media.write: to upload and post the account's own videos.
  • tweet.read: to check the account's recent posts so a video is never posted twice.
  • offline.access: to stay signed in between posts without asking again.

How we use it

Only to publish and check the channels' own videos, when the operator asks for it or at a time they scheduled. We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where it is stored

  • Sign-ins (access and refresh tokens) and app credentials are stored only on the operator's computer, in the operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain or Windows DPAPI). They are never written to files, logs or source code.
  • Records of published posts (title, caption, platform post ID and link) are kept in a local database and in the operator's private source repository.
  • Media Studio has no servers or cloud storage of its own.

Sharing

We do not sell or share account data with anyone. Media Studio only communicates with the platform each video is posted to: Google (YouTube Data API), Meta (Instagram and Facebook Graph API, Threads API), TikTok (Content Posting API) and X (X API).

Threads only accepts a video from a public link. For a Threads post, the video is therefore placed briefly on GitHub (a temporary release in the public media-studio-site repository) and deleted as soon as Threads has fetched it. Only the video file is shared this way, never account data.

The sign-in pages on this site (meta-callback.html, threads-callback.html, tiktok-callback.html) only pass the one-time sign-in code from the platform to Media Studio on the operator's computer. They store nothing and load no third-party scripts.

Media Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

A sign-in is kept until the operator disconnects that platform in Media Studio, which deletes it from the computer (for YouTube and X it is also revoked with the platform). Access can also be removed at any time on the platform:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites
  • Threads: Threads Settings → Account → Website permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps

Post records are kept as the channels' publishing history and can be deleted on request.

Children

The Blip & Friends videos are made for children, but Media Studio does not collect any information from children or other viewers. Viewing happens on each platform, under its own rules and privacy policy. On YouTube, these videos are marked "made for kids"; on TikTok, comments are turned off for them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Contact us at the address above.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.
