Privacy Policy

Media Studio · Effective 2026-10-13

Who we are

Media Studio is a private tool that the operator of the Blip & Friends YouTube channel runs on their own computer to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

Data controller: Blip & Friends, the Netherlands. Contact: highvibes101.101@gmail.com.

Google user data we access

When the operator connects their YouTube account, Media Studio asks Google for two permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy, "made for kids" and AI-disclosure settings.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to look up the connected channel's most recent uploads, so that a video is never uploaded twice after an interrupted upload.

Media Studio does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, email, contacts or any other Google service.

How we use it

Only to upload and check the channel's own videos, when the operator asks it to. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where it is stored

  • The Google sign-in (refresh token) and the app's OAuth client are stored only on the operator's computer, in the operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain or Windows DPAPI). Short-lived access tokens are kept in memory and never written to disk.
  • Records of published posts (title, caption, video ID and link) are kept in a local database and in the operator's private source repository.
  • Media Studio has no servers or cloud storage of its own.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone. The only party Media Studio communicates with is Google, through the YouTube Data API.

Media Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The sign-in is kept until the operator disconnects YouTube in Media Studio, which revokes it with Google and deletes it from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Post records are kept as the channel's publishing history and can be deleted on request.

Children

The Blip & Friends videos are made for children, but Media Studio does not collect any information from children or other viewers. Viewing happens on YouTube, under YouTube's "made for kids" rules and Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Contact us at the address above.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.
