Media Studio

The publishing tool behind Blip & Friends

Media Studio is a private tool that the operator of the Blip & Friends YouTube channel uses to make short cartoon episodes and upload them to the channel. It runs on the operator's own computer and is not offered to the public.

It connects to YouTube with Google sign-in only to upload the channel's own videos and to check that each video was uploaded once. It does not collect data from viewers.
