Media Studio

The publishing tool behind Blip & Friends

Media Studio is a private tool that the operator of the Blip & Friends channels uses to make short cartoon episodes and publish them to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, X and Pinterest. It runs on the operator's own computer and is not offered to the public.

It signs in to the channels' own accounts only to publish their own videos and to check that each video was posted once. It does not collect data from viewers.
